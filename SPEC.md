# ITB Wildlife: design rules

Goal: bring real animal behaviour to Skyrim. One standalone mod. Masters: the five official files (Skyrim, Update, Dawnguard, HearthFires, Dragonborn) and Creation Club files only. Nothing else, ever.

## Content scope
- Animals, dragons, atronachs and spriggans: full content.
- Undead: allowed, but only where vanilla puts dead NPCs (dungeons and crypts). No outdoor spawning, no wild schedules. Variants of the undead types are welcome there.
- Trolls: keep their behaviours. Any spawn point that is not vanilla goes only where trolls belong: mountains, dens, caves and similar.
- Giants: vanilla spawn points only.
- Atronachs and spriggans: spawn exactly like vanilla.
- Daedra and daedra-like creatures (dremora, hounds and so on): out.
- Extra dragons: only after the game's dragons begin to wake from their mounds (main quest progress), never before.

## Animals
- Every animal species gets a young variant (pup, cub, foal, fawn, calf and so on): smaller body, lower stats, and it stays close to its mother.
- Horse herds stay as already built and tested.
- Wolves hunt in packs of 3 to 5 adults, normally with pups.
- Bears spawn by type in the right regions: black bears, brown bears and snow bears are never mixed.
- Real-life behaviour is the target: daily routines, feeding, resting, herding, mating seasons, hibernation, fear of fire, predators and prey.
- Prey flees, predators hunt. A predator-avoidance mechanic like the one used in the earlier work is kept (or a close equivalent): prey and small animals clear out when a predator or the player comes close.
- Stagger and knockdown are capped at vanilla (5% of vanilla for the big predators, no knockdown), so a pack can never keep the player on the ground.

## Player horse
The tamed or given horse gets a unique name, drawn from a Nordic, lore-friendly list.

## Naming and files
- Everything is renamed and renumbered: editor IDs `ITB_`, new FormIDs, new asset folders and file names, nothing that resembles an older mod anywhere in plugin, assets, scripts or text.
- The plugin has no master except the official and Creation Club files.
